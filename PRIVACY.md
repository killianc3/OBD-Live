# Privacy Policy

  **OBD Live** is built with your privacy in mind and does not collect any information that identifies you personally.

  _Last updated: 6 September 2026_

  ## Data processed locally (never transmitted)

  - Communication with your vehicle happens only over **Bluetooth** with the ELM327 adapter.
  - **Sensor values**, recordings and drives are stored **only on your device** (JSON format, timestamped), for live
  display, history, replay and diagnostics.
  - **Location (GPS)** and **motion (accelerometer/gyroscope)** data, when you grant permission, are used **only on the
  device** (drive route, distance calculation, segment detection) and are **never sent to a server**.

  This data is never shared, sold, or used for advertising.

  ## Anonymous usage statistics

  To understand which features are used and to fix problems, the app sends **anonymous usage statistics** to the
  third-party service **Aptabase** (hosted in the European Union), a privacy-friendly analytics tool: no cookies, no IP
  address stored, no device identifier.

  **What is sent:**
  - **interaction events** (opening a screen, starting a drive, viewing a trouble code, etc.);
  - **bucketed indicators** ("1–3", "under 10s"…) — never precise values;
  - the **app version**, **OS version**, and **language**;
  - a **temporary session identifier** (random, renewed after one hour of inactivity, not tied to an account or a
  device);
  - if a component fails, an **error message class** (short text, no file paths).

  **What is never sent:** your name, email, GPS position, your vehicle identifier (VIN) or adapter identifier, or any
  raw sensor data.

  This data is **not linked to your identity** and is **not used for tracking** (no sharing with a data broker, no
  combining with third-party data, no targeted advertising).

  **Opt out:** any time, in **Settings → Anonymous usage stats**.

  Aptabase privacy policy: https://aptabase.com/legal/privacy

  ## Maps

  The drive map screen downloads map tiles from **OpenStreetMap** servers. These requests may cause your IP address to
  appear in OpenStreetMap's logs, per their own policy. No content from your drive is sent to them.

  ## Security

  Apart from the anonymous statistics and map tiles described above, no data leaves your device.

  ## Contact

  For any question about this policy or data handling, contact the developer through the app's support page.
