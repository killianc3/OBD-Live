Setup

Up and running in 3 steps.

All cars manufactured after 1996 have an OBD2 port. A cheap ELM327 adapter is all you need.

Plug the adapter
Insert any ELM327 Bluetooth adapter into the OBD2 port under your dashboard — usually near the steering column.
Pair via Bluetooth
Open OBD Live and tap the adapter name when it appears. The app handles the connection automatically.
Explore your car
Pick a dataset, start monitoring live sensors, read fault codes or record a session. Everything in one tap.

What it does

Everything your garage needs.

No subscription, no account, no cloud. Your phone, an ELM327 adapter, and your car.

Live sensor data
Monitor RPM, coolant temperature, MAF, O2 sensors and hundreds of other OBD2 PIDs with smooth real-time charts.
Trouble codes
Read stored, pending and permanent DTCs. View freeze-frame data at the time of fault. Clear codes in one tap.
Session recording
Record any combination of sensors and replay the session later. Perfect for tracking intermittent issues over time.
